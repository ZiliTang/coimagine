Experimental condition: CAP with explicit task-effect modules.
Organize one centralized program around reusable closed-loop task-effect modules.
Do not make 'establish contact' a stand-alone mandatory macro: PUSH already handles
local contact acquisition. Instead implement these parameterized responsibilities:

1. TransportSegment: move the object towards an estimated local target/region,
   handling the joint option choices, progress monitoring and bounded recovery
   needed for that segment. It can serve free-space transport and individual legs
   of an obstacle bypass. Support final arrival and settling as part of this module
   or a closely associated terminal phase, rather than equating distance alone
   with the public success rule.
2. ReleaseForTransport: change a wall-constrained configuration into one from which
   a requested transport segment can proceed. Infer constraint and release from
   legal sensing and observed effects. Rotation is one possible means, not a
   mandatory solution or sufficient evidence of release. The exit contract must
   address the next transport segment, not just an angle change or robot contact.
3. BypassObstacle: choose and follow a sequence of local transport targets around
   an observed fixed obstruction, accounting for the manipulated object's extent
   and robot execution access. Reuse TransportSegment where appropriate and verify
   the passage and handoff to the goal segment. A point path existing is not by
   itself a completed bypass. Select your own map representation and route method.

A supervisor selects/re-enters these modules from observations and history, never
from a task/family ID. Modules may be nested or combined where justified. Keep
their responsibilities inspectable in the submitted source and DIAGNOSTIC.json.
For each module define: observable entry conditions; parameters/local objective;
within-module state; a policy producing legal joint options; observed progress;
success/handoff conditions; and bounded failure/abort conditions. On failure return
an explicit reason to the supervisor rather than silently declaring completion.
Choose all numerical thresholds, action sequences and recovery behaviors yourself
using the same development budget. This is a researcher's architecture prompt,
not a supplied controller implementation, experience library or world model.
